Documentation

How this dashboard thinks about water shortage

The Pakistan Water Shortage Predictor pairs a gradient-boosting rainfall model with the standard FAO water-balance approach to produce a Water Sufficiency Index (WSI) for every supported district and crop. It is intended as decision support — a quantitative second opinion alongside on-ground observation, not a substitute for it.

Methodology

01

Predict monthly rainfall

The model predicts millimetres of rainfall for the chosen district, month, and year using eight features: previous-month rainfall, cyclical month encoding (sin/cos), year, temperature, humidity, wind speed, and solar radiation. Missing weather inputs fall back to the historical district mean for that month.

02

Derive effective water supply

Not all rainfall is usable: some runs off, some percolates beyond the root zone. A soil-retention factor scales the prediction. Any supplemental irrigation the user reports is added on top.

effective_supply = (rainfall × soil_factor) + irrigation
03

Compute the Water Sufficiency Index

Crop water demand is taken from the dataset (where present) or imputed as the average for that crop. The WSI is the ratio of supply to demand, expressed as a percentage, then mapped to a five-level severity class.

WSI = (effective_supply / crop_water_demand) × 100

The R² is intentionally reported as-is. Monthly rainfall is intrinsically hard to forecast from a small feature set, and the model is most useful as a relative ranking across districts and months rather than as a precise meteorological prediction. Improving skill is a roadmap item — satellite NDVI, ENSO indices, and longer history windows are the most promising additions.

warning Disclaimer

Outputs are probabilistic estimates based on historical data and a machine-learning model whose R² on held-out data is low. They are intended as decision support, not as statutory forecasts of water availability. Cropping, irrigation, and emergency-response decisions should always be made in consultation with local agricultural extension services and verified by on-ground observation.
